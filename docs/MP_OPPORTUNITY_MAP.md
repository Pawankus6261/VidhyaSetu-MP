## 2. Institutional Pilot Recommendations

### Primary Pilot Site 1: Western Tribal Corridor
* **College:** **Shaheed Bheema Nayak Government Post Graduate College, Barwani**
* **University Affiliation:** Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), Indore.
* **Why Selected:** Represents the highest concentration of Bhil/Bhilala and Nimadi-speaking undergraduate students in Western MP. Established faculty nodal office for MPTAAS; existing enrollment of 3,800+ students.

### Primary Pilot Site 2: Eastern Tribal Corridor
* **College:** **Government Chandra Vijay College, Dindori**
* **University Affiliation:** Rani Durgavati Vishwavidyalaya (RDVV), Jabalpur.
* **Why Selected:** Deep forest tribal district bordering Amarkantak. Significant Gond and Baiga PVTG population experiencing the lowest measured cellular data speeds in Eastern MP (averaging 18–30 kbps). Ideal testbed for zero-byte offline performance.
