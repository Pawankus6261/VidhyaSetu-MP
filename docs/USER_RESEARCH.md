### Persona 1: Rural Undergraduate Student (The Migrant Scholar)
* **Name:** Rakesh Bhil (Age 19)
* **Location:** Village Sondwa, Tehsil Alirajpur, District Alirajpur, MP.
* **Academic Profile:** 1st Year B.A. (History, Political Science, Hindi Literature), Govt. Degree College Sondwa.
* **Device & Network:** Shared family Redmi 9A (32GB storage, 2GB RAM); Jio 1.5 GB/day plan (exhausted by 2 PM by younger siblings; zero signal inside mud-brick home, requires walking 400m to hilltop tree for 1-bar 4G).
* **Linguistic Reality:** Speaks Bhili at home; conversational Hindi for market; cannot write formal Hindi academic essays without grammatical errors; zero English comprehension.
* **Goals:** Clear 1st-year exams without backlogs; avoid dropping out to migrate to Gujarat as farm labor.
* **Frustrations:** Professors dictate notes rapidly in college; missed classes due to harvesting season cannot be made up; cannot afford private tuition (₹500/subject).

### Persona 2: Tribal-Region Undergraduate Student (PVTG Adjacent)
* **Name:** Sunita Maravi (Age 20)
* **Location:** Village Samnapur, District Dindori, MP.
* **Academic Profile:** 2nd Year B.Sc. (Botany, Zoology, Chemistry), Govt. Chandra Vijay College, Dindori.
* **Device & Network:** Pre-owned Realme C11 (32GB, 2GB RAM, cracked screen); BSNL 3G SIM (drops to 0 kbps for 3–4 days every monsoon); battery charged using solar inverter.
* **Linguistic Reality:** Gondi native speaker; understands spoken Hindi; struggles deeply with English scientific nomenclature (e.g., Angiosperms, Photosynthesis mechanisms).
* **Goals:** Become a government school lab assistant or teacher; secure Awas Sahayata scholarship to pay for room rent in Dindori.
* **Frustrations:** Library has only 2 copies of Hindi-medium chemistry textbooks; YouTube science lectures speak in rapid Hinglish or pure English with dense mathematical diagrams.
