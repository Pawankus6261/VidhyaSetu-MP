## 1. Grounding Career Discovery in Rural Economic Realities

Traditional EdTech career counselling promotes high-cost urban career paths (e.g., Software Engineering in Bengaluru, Investment Banking in Mumbai) that require high upfront capital, migration expenses, and elite English communication skills.

In rural Madhya Pradesh, **over 92% of collegiate students cannot afford metropolitan migration**. VidyaSetu MP structures career discovery around **Five Realistic Economic Horizons**:

## 2. Interactive Offline Decision Tree Logic

```
[Start: Student Profile in Barwani / Dindori]
  │
  ├── Step 1: Migration Preference
  │     ├── Can migrate to Indore/Bhopal? ──► Explore Regional Corporate & State Civil Services
  │     └── Must stay in home district?   ──► Branch into Local Economic Clusters
  │
  ├── Step 2: Time Horizon to Earning
  │     ├── Immediate (<6 months required):
  │     │     └── Pathway: Bank Business Correspondent (BC) / Common Service Centre (CSC)
  │     │           • Requirements: IIBF BC Certificate exam, 10th/12th Marksheet
  │     │           • Earning: ₹9,000–₹16,000/month in home tehsil
  │     │           • Offline Modules: Basic digital bookkeeping, Biometric POS terminal handling
  │     │
  │     ├── Medium-Term (6–18 months):
  │     │     └── Pathway: MP State Government Recruitment (ESB / Vyapam)
  │     │           • Target: Forest Guard (Vanrakshak) / Police Constable
  │     │           • Requirements: 10th/12th, Physical fitness criteria
  │     │           • Offline Modules: Daily 15-minute General Hindi & MP GK audio lessons
  │     │
  │     └── Long-Term (18–36 months):
  │           └── Pathway: Higher Education & Specialized Academic Research
  │                 • Target: M.Sc. / M.A. at Indira Gandhi National Tribal University (IGNTU)
  │                 • Entitlements: UGC National Fellowship for ST Students (NFST)
  │                 • Offline Modules: CUET-PG syllabus breakdown & previous exam archives
```
