## 2. Granular Milestone Deliverables

### Milestone 1: Hackathon Core Engine (Current)
* Functional proof of offline-first learning on budget Android devices.
* Working demonstration of **1.8 MB Audio-Slide Micro-Packs** at 0 kbps.
* Deterministic offline evaluation of MPTAAS Post-Matric and Gaon Ki Beti scholarships.
* Asynchronous Brotli-compressed outbox queue and cloud curriculum RAG resolution.

### Milestone 2: Field College Validation (Months 3–6)
* Deployment at **Shaheed Bheema Nayak Govt PG College, Barwani** and **Govt Chandra Vijay College, Dindori**.
* Telemetry logging on cellular bandwidth consumption and packet loss patterns under real rural 2G/3G network conditions.
* Real-world validation of dialect phonological normalization across Bhili and Gondi student cohorts.

### Milestone 3: Statewide Tribal Block Scale (Months 7–12)
* Formal rollout backed by the **Department of Tribal Affairs, Govt of MP**.
* Authoring of full 3-year undergraduate syllabi for B.A., B.Sc., and B.Com in partnership with the **Madhya Pradesh Hindi Granth Academy**.
* College Principal & Scholarship Nodal Officer dashboard for tracking pending MPTAAS document discrepancies.

### Milestone 4: National Replication & Viksit Bharat Scale (Years 2–3)
* Expansion of the open-core architecture to neighboring tribal corridors (Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Odisha, Rajasthan).
* Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) integration for micro-credential recording.
* Offline P2P mesh synchronization enabling content exchange in weekly village *haat-bazaars* without cellular tower dependency.
