## 2. Institutional Pilot Partners in Madhya Pradesh

1. **Shaheed Bheema Nayak Government Post Graduate College, Barwani:**
   * Focus: Western Tribal Belt (Bhil & Bhilala communities, Nimadi vernacular context).
   * Pilot Target: 250 1st-Year B.A. & B.Sc. students.
2. **Government Chandra Vijay College, Dindori:**
   * Focus: Eastern Tribal Belt (Gond community and Baiga PVTG, Bagheli/Gondi context).
   * Pilot Target: 250 1st-Year B.A. & B.Com students.
3. **Indira Gandhi National Tribal University (IGNTU), Amarkantak:**
   * Focus: Higher research mentorship, academic review, and tribal curriculum authoring.
4. **Department of Tribal Affairs, Government of MP:**
   * Focus: Official validation of MPTAAS scholarship rule schemas and nodal officer dashboard integration.

---

## 3. Measurable Pilot Success Criteria

* **Academic Retention:** Minimum **20% improvement** in 1st-year examination pass percentages in pilot cohorts compared to historical college controls.
* **Scholarship Success Rate:** **>90%** of eligible students in pilot groups successfully receiving MPTAAS/Gaon Ki Beti disbursements without portal rejection.
* **Bandwidth Savings:** Minimum **85% reduction** in cellular data consumption compared to students using standard YouTube/online resources.
* **App Stability:** Zero crash reports on Android Go 2GB RAM test devices over a 60-day continuous field cycle.
