## 2. United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Alignment

### SDG 4: Quality Education
* **Target 4.3 (Equal Access to Technical/Vocational/Tertiary Education):** Ensures youth in Fifth Schedule tribal areas have equal access to affordable and quality higher education without metropolitan migration.
* **Target 4.5 (Eliminate Gender & Vulnerable Disparities):** Eliminates gender access barriers through offline Gaon Ki Beti discovery and asynchronous study that accommodates female agrarian/household labor schedules.
* **Target 4.c (Substantially Increase Supply of Qualified Teachers):** Equips overworked rural college professors (1:180+ faculty ratios) with automated Tier-0 and Tier-1 doubt triage tools, multiplying their pedagogical reach.

### SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth
* **Target 8.6 (Substantially Reduce Youth Not in Employment, Education, or Training - NEET):** The career decision engine guides collegiate youth directly into certified local livelihoods (Bank Business Correspondents, agro-processing, renewable solar maintenance).

### SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities
* **Target 10.2 (Empower and Promote Social, Economic and Political Inclusion):** Dismantles the digital divide separating urban, high-speed, English-fluent students from rural, dialect-speaking students.
* **Target 10.3 (Ensure Equal Opportunity and Reduce Inequalities of Outcome):** Resolves bureaucratic scholarship leakage (MPTAAS), ensuring statutory welfare funds reach the most marginalized ST/SC/BPL households.
